# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This project is a place to build a tool for thinking, and everyone who takes part should be able to do that without being harassed.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat others with respect. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith, give specific and actionable feedback, and accept it the same way.
- Keep issues, pull requests and reviews on topic.
- Be patient with newcomers; answer the question that was asked.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks, in any form.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeated pressure to merge or review a change.

## Scope

This applies to every space the project runs: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and any channel the maintainers announce.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may ask a person to stop taking part, temporarily or for good. Decisions follow the process in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

To report a problem, use the private contact named under "Conduct and reporting contact" in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Reports are handled in confidence, and the person who reports is not exposed to the person reported.

Contact for conduct reports: `conduct@example.com`. This is a placeholder: the maintainer replaces it with a real, monitored address before the repository goes public.
