# Senior ML Engineer, Perception — Acme Robotics

Location: Tel Aviv (hybrid, 3 days on site)
Posted: 2026-09-24

## About Acme Robotics
Acme Robotics builds autonomous warehouse robots. Our perception team turns camera, lidar and IMU streams into a live map of the floor so that fleets of 400+ robots can move safely around people.

## What you will do
- Own the training and evaluation pipeline for our 3D object detection and tracking models.
- Fuse lidar, camera and IMU signals into a single robust state estimate (sensor fusion).
- Ship models to the edge: quantization, latency budgets, on-robot profiling.
- Work with the ML-ops group on retraining triggers, dataset versioning and shadow deployments.

## Requirements
- 5+ years of applied machine learning, 2+ in perception or robotics.
- Strong PyTorch; comfortable writing custom CUDA-free ops and data loaders.
- Experience designing ML systems end to end (system design interview is part of the loop).
- Practical ML-ops: CI for models, monitoring, drift detection.
- Clear communicator; you can explain a failure to a mechanical engineer.

## Nice to have
- Kalman filtering, factor graphs, SLAM.
- Publications at CVPR, ICRA or similar.

## Process
Recruiter screen, technical interview with a senior engineer, system-design round, hiring manager conversation, offer.
