# Applied Scientist, Clinical NLP — Nimbus Health

Location: Tel Aviv or remote (Israel)
Posted: 2026-10-05

## About Nimbus Health
Nimbus Health builds software that turns doctors' free-text notes into structured data for hospitals. We process 2 million clinical notes a month in Hebrew and English.

## The role
You will lead modelling work on clinical named-entity recognition, negation detection and note summarisation, and decide with clinicians which errors matter most.

## Responsibilities
- Fine-tune and evaluate transformer models on de-identified clinical text.
- Design annotation guidelines together with physicians.
- Build evaluation sets that reflect rare but dangerous cases.
- Present results to the hospital partners' medical staff.

## Requirements
- MSc or PhD in a quantitative field, or equivalent experience.
- Hands-on clinical or biomedical NLP.
- Fluent PyTorch and the Hugging Face stack.
- Good at telling a story about past projects (behavioural interview with the hiring manager, Maya Cohen).

## Nice to have
- Hebrew NLP, privacy-preserving training, active learning.
