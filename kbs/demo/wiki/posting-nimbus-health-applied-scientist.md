# Applied Scientist, Clinical NLP (Nimbus Health)

**Company:** [[company-nimbus-health]] · **Role:** [[role-applied-scientist-nimbus]] · **Posted:** 2026-10-05, Tel Aviv or remote.

**What it was about:** Nimbus turns clinicians' free-text notes into structured data for hospitals, processing about 2 million notes a month in Hebrew and English. The scientist leads modelling for clinical entity recognition, negation detection and summarisation, and decides with physicians which errors matter most.

## Key claims
- Hands-on clinical or biomedical NLP is required ([[skill-clinical-nlp]]), with fluent [[skill-pytorch]] and the Hugging Face stack.
- Evaluation sets must cover rare but dangerous cases; annotation guidelines are written together with physicians.
- The hiring-manager conversation is explicitly behavioural, so stories matter ([[skill-interviewing-stories]]); the manager is [[maya-cohen]].
- Hebrew NLP, privacy-preserving training and active learning are nice to have.

## Takeaways
A strong fit on NLP and PyTorch, thinner on clinical specifics. See [[process-nimbus-health]].
