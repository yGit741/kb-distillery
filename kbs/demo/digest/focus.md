# Digest focus — job search

The point of this knowledge base is deciding where to apply and preparing for each conversation.

- From a posting, pull out the role, the company and the skills they actually test; link each as a concept.
- From a recruiter mail, record the stage, the dates and the people, and update the company's hiring process.
- From an interview-prep chat or interview notes, keep what was asked and what worked; feed skill pages.
- Keep source pages faithful; opinions ("this team looks strong") go in `## My notes`.
